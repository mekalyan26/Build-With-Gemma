CareFlow 360 is a clinician-facing medical assistant that turns patient history and image inputs into concise, actionable summaries using AI.

It combines a polished Streamlit UI with a PostgreSQL-backed patient and AI response store for reliable data persistence and audit trails.

The app uses Spur/OpenAI chat completions to generate medically structured summaries, then captures clinician feedback as Accepted, Needs update, or Denied.

A dashboard surface shows patient quick stats, ages, and AI response remarks so teams can monitor AI quality and identify cases needing review.

This creates measurable impact by accelerating case review, improving transparency, and closing the loop between AI suggestions and clinician validation.
